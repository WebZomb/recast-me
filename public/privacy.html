Photo privacy & help

Updated October 3, 2026

Your photos and AI

When you choose Create Preview and give permission, Recast Me sends your selected photos and written descriptions to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate artwork. Photos and artwork are stored in private Cloudflare R2 storage; Cloudflare Images processes previews and print files.

Only upload photos you have permission to use. Results can change faces, markings and details. Check your preview before choosing a product. Customer photos are not used in our advertising without separate permission.

Previews and orders

Customer previews have a watermark. Clean digital files require an eligible paid digital purchase. Physical fulfillment uses the selected clean artwork. Shopify processes checkout and order information. Printful receives the artwork and delivery information needed to produce and ship a physical order.

Keep private artwork and order links to yourself. They provide access to your Recast. Use Save / share watermarked preview to share the image without sharing your private link.

Storage and deletion

This browser keeps your four most recent successful Recast references and private access links. Clearing browser data can remove access to them. When preview allowances are enabled, a browser cookie and a salted network identifier help manage credits and prevent repeated free-trial resets.

To delete an unpaid Recast, open its Track this Recast / downloads page and choose Delete this unpaid Recast. Paid artwork may need to be retained for fulfillment and order support. Automatic deletion after a fixed number of days is not currently promised.

In the iPhone development app, My Recasts saves only watermarked preview copies on the device. Removing a local copy does not delete its server artwork or cancel an order.

Need help?

Contact @RecastMeAi on X with a general description of the problem. Do not post your private order link, access token, address, or payment information. A dedicated order-support contact and final shipping/refund terms must be verified before public launch.
