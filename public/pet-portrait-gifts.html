Choose a portrait style that suits their personality

A Royal portrait gives a familiar face a regal setting. Space Explorer suits a curious adventurer, while Fantasy Warrior creates a more dramatic scene. Start with the same clear pet photo when comparing styles so you can judge the eyes, markings and expression. An AI transformation is an interpretation: check the result before putting it on a gift.

The best starting photo

Choose one well-lit image with the eyes and face in focus. Include distinctive ears and markings, and avoid a heavily filtered screenshot or a distant pet in a busy scene. For a portrait of two pets, make sure both faces are visible and review both in the finished image. You can change the suggested subject after uploading.

Mug, canvas or pillow?

A personalized pet mug makes the portrait part of an everyday routine. Canvas gives the artwork a place on the wall; consider where it will hang before choosing a size. A pillow works as a decorative keepsake for a favorite chair. Compare the product preview and crop rather than assuming the same composition fits every shape.

Keep the original for a memorial keepsake

If preserving the exact photograph matters most, choose Use My Photo instead of an AI style. This is also an option for a pet memorial gift. Keep the face clear of product edges and review the placement before checkout. Upload only a photograph you have permission to use.
