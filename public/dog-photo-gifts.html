Pick a world for their personality

Royal suits a dignified companion; Space Explorer gives a curious dog a cosmic adventure. Fantasy Warrior brings a bold scene, while Storybook offers a softer illustrated look. Holiday Magic adds a seasonal setting. These are starting ideas: browse all available styles in the creator and review your dog’s face, coat and markings before choosing a gift.

Choose a gift they will use

For a daily coffee drinker, compare a dog-photo mug or tumbler. For their favorite corner at home, try a blanket or pillow. Canvas, posters and framed prints turn the portrait into wall art. T-shirts and hoodies put the dog portrait on clothing; tote bags and phone cases are other ways to carry the picture. Check the available product sizes and placement in the main shop.

A good dog photo makes the difference

Choose a well-lit photograph with sharp eyes and a visible muzzle. Leave space around tall ears and long fur. For a black or white coat, avoid lighting that hides the face or washes out markings. A single clear photo is a better starting point than a collage. With two dogs, check that both remain recognizable after an AI transformation.

Keep the real memory

Use My Photo keeps the original photograph instead of applying an AI transformation. It can be a thoughtful choice for an adoption anniversary or memorial keepsake. Product fitting can still crop the photograph, so inspect the edges and placement in the preview. Use only photos you have permission to use.
