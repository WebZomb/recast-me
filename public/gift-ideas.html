Choose the right photo gift

Mugs & tumblers: a useful everyday gift; check each product preview angle to see the photo placement.

Blankets, pillows & tote bags: keep the whole picture with background fill, or fill the product and review the crop. Keep faces away from seams and edges.

T-shirts & hoodies: wear a pet portrait or favorite adventure. Check the selected size and front placement in the preview.

Phone cases & wall art: take a favorite photo with you or display it at home. Check the phone model and camera cutout; review the crop and size for prints.
