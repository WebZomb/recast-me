Original memory or illustrated adventure?

A family photograph keeps a real moment at the center of the blanket. A pet portrait in Royal, Storybook or Space Explorer style adds a playful setting. Choose Use My Photo for the original photograph, or create and review an AI Recast first. Either way, check the picture before moving to the blanket preview.

Compose for a larger picture

Choose a clear image with some room around the subject. A photo that looks sharp as a tiny phone thumbnail may not show the same detail when enlarged. Keep faces, ears and meaningful text away from the outer edges. For group photos, check every person and leave enough space around the group for the selected product shape.

Compare the crop and selected size

The blanket preview helps you judge the composition for the selected option. Review the whole blanket, not just the center of the artwork. Compare fit and fill controls where offered, and check the size shown in the shop before ordering. A close crop may suit one pet portrait while a wider composition suits a family scene.

Choosing a gift for someone else

For a dog lover, use a recognizable portrait of their pet. For parents or grandparents, a clear family photograph can be a personal choice. A matching photo mug or pillow offers another way to use the memory. For date-specific gifts, read the current delivery information at checkout; this guide does not promise a holiday arrival date.
