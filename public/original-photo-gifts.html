When to use your original photo

Choose Use My Photo when the photograph itself is the memory: a family gathering, a couple on an anniversary, a much-loved pet or a favorite car. This option skips the AI artwork transformation and takes your picture into product previews. Product fitting may still crop the image, so review the selected layout.

Choose the photo before the product

Use the clearest original file you have rather than a small screenshot or a picture downloaded from a messaging preview. A wide group photograph needs room around every person; a close portrait often works better on a tall print. Avoid placing important faces or text at the very edge of the source picture.

Match the gift to the moment

A photo mug suits a daily coffee ritual. A blanket or pillow gives a family picture a place at home, while a canvas or framed print makes the photograph the focus of a wall. For a birthday or anniversary gift, choose a picture the recipient already loves. Compare the actual sizes and available options in the main shop.

Check the fit before checkout

Open the product preview and review faces, cropping, placement and the selected size. Where fit and fill options are offered, compare them: filling the area can trim edges, while keeping the whole picture can leave space around it. Preview watermarks do not print on purchased products. Review the current checkout delivery information when buying for a particular date.
