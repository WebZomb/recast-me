Photo privacy

Customer photos are used for the requested transformation, support, and fulfillment. Recast Me does not use customer photos in advertising without separate explicit opt-in.

Results vary. Recast Me creates original aesthetic directions and does not intentionally reproduce protected logos, branded characters, signature title treatments, or third-party marketing artwork. Harmful or exploitative trend requests are rejected or routed to review.

Custom product / refund policy

Personalized products cannot generally be restocked. Damaged, defective, or incorrectly produced items are eligible for review and reprint/replacement. Final launch policy will be shown before paid checkout is enabled.
