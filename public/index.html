Photo privacy

Customer photos are for the requested transformation, support, and fulfillment only. Recast Me will not use customer photos in advertising without separate explicit opt-in. The production system will include deletion controls before launch.

AI & image policy

Results vary. Recast Me creates original aesthetic directions and does not intentionally reproduce protected logos, branded characters, signature title treatments, or third-party marketing artwork. Harmful or exploitative trend requests are rejected or sent to human review.

Custom product / refund policy

Personalized products cannot generally be restocked. Damaged, defective, or incorrectly produced items will be eligible for review and reprint/replacement. The full policy will be finalized before paid checkout goes live.
