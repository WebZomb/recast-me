Choose a look for their cat

Royal gives a cat a regal setting; Storybook is a gentle illustrated option. Space Explorer suits a playful cosmic scene, and Film Noir offers a cinematic mood. For seasonal artwork, explore Holiday Magic. Review the result for the cat’s eye color, facial shape and distinctive coat pattern; style choices are available in the main creator.

Start with clear eyes and whiskers

Photograph the cat at eye level in soft, even light. Keep pointed ears and whiskers within the frame and avoid a tiny face photographed from across the room. If the cat has a dark coat, choose a photo where the eyes and muzzle are still easy to distinguish. For multiple cats, use a picture with each face clearly visible and inspect every subject in the result.

Match the product to their home and routine

A cat-photo mug or tumbler is an everyday option. A pillow makes a decorative accent; a blanket gives the portrait more space. Canvas and framed prints work for a favorite wall. T-shirts, hoodies, tote bags and phone cases offer wearable or portable alternatives. Compare the selected size and crop in the product preview before deciding.

For the photograph they already love

Choose Use My Photo to keep a favorite sleeping pose, kitten picture or memorial photograph without an AI transformation. Keep the face away from seams, product edges and phone-camera cutouts. Preview watermarks do not print on purchased products. The shop shows the current available options.
